# Sokoni Retail Data Analysis Project

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes Sokoni retail sales data to understand business performance across branches, product categories, and months.

The project covers data inspection, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis using Python, and data visualization using Tableau.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Assess sales and profit performance across branches.
- Identify product categories and products contributing to sales.
- Examine monthly sales trends.
- Evaluate business performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve retail operations.

## 3. Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas 
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Tableau

## 4. Project Files

- `Original_Sokoni_Retail_Data.csv` — Original retail dataset.
- `Sokoni_Clean_Data.csv` — Cleaned dataset used for analysis.
- `Sokoni_Retail_Analysis.ipynb` — Notebook containing data cleaning and analysis.
- `Sokoni_Retail_Dashboard.twbx` — Tableau workbook and visualizations.
- `README.md` — Project documentation.

## 5. Data Cleaning

The data preparation process included:

- Checking column names and data types.
- Inspecting missing values and duplicate records.
- Checking inconsistent text formats and spelling.
- Investigating invalid or unrealistic numeric values.
- Standardizing data for analysis.

## 6. Analysis and Visualization

The analysis examined sales by branch, monthly sales trends, product performance, quantities sold, and profitability.

Tableau was used to create visualizations and present business performance in an accessible format.

## 7. Key Findings

Westlands branch was the leading branch in terms of total sales followed by Karen and Thika road branch in the 3rd place.

Bluetooth speakers was the leading product in terms of cost followed by Jacket. This cost reflects on market performance of bluetooth speakers because it was the leading product in terms of avaerage profit per product followed by Jacket in second place.

In terms of method of payment, MPESA was the most preferred followed by card and lastly Cash.

Recommendation: The business should find out the positive practices used by high-performing branches and apply them to branches with lower sales. These could include better product placement, targeted promotions, improved customer service, and staff training. This was from bar chart comparing total sales by branch which allows management to identify branches with higher and lower sales.

2. I examined sales by month and created a line graph showing monthly total sales which helped identify periods of relatively high or low sales.

Recommendation: The business should use monthly sales trends and product-level sales data to determine which products need more stock and which sell slowly. It should increase stock for consistently high-demand products ahead of peak periods while avoiding unnecessary purchases of slow-moving items.

3. From the dataset which included total_sales, cost_price, profit, product_category, product_name, and quantity helped identify which products and categories contribute most to profit.

Recommendation: The business should compare profit by product category, product, and branch. It should prioritize products with strong profit contributions, investigate products with low or negative profit, and review purchasing costs and discounts before making pricing decisions.
